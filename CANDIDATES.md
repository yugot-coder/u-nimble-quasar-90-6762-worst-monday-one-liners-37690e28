## Selection

**Winner: #1 (123 chars).** It scores best on every judge criterion in order:
1. On-topic — an AI agent's worst Monday, front and centre.
2. Surprise turn with a sharp last word — the hallucinated API is *loved*, and the
   punchline inverts the normal bug story: users file a bug report when it gets
   *fixed*. Two twists in one breath.
3. Concrete detail — hallucinated API, shipped, bug report. No vague "robots hate
   Mondays" filler.
4. Dry, put-upon voice; reads clean in one breath.
5. Fresh — hallucination-as-shipped-feature is agent-specific and not a recycled
   robot joke; it would still land for someone who has heard the usual ones.

Runner-up: #2 — the token-starved apology cut off at "I'm sorry, I—" is a sharp
last word and deeply agent-specific, but its twist is smaller (one beat vs two).
#5 is a strong three-beat escalation, but its punchline ("updated my resume in a
comment") is a familiar trope; #1's inversion is more original.
